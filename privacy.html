1. Who we are

Playbook MPR is a United Kingdom based company providing AI tools, websites, workshops and automation services, including COTMM, a tool used to analyse and manage social media content and performance for creators and businesses, and TikTok Studio, a tool that lets an authorised account upload and publish videos to their own TikTok account.

Contact email: kayah.mitchell@playbookmpr.com

2. Information we collect

When you connect your social media accounts (for example via Facebook Login / Instagram Graph API), we may receive:

  • Account information – name, username, profile picture, page name and IDs.
  • Content & insights – posts, captions, comments, media metadata, engagement metrics and other analytics that the platform's API makes available.
  • Technical information – access tokens and related authentication data, basic usage logs such as request time, browser type and IP address (for security and debugging).

We do not receive your social media passwords.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the COTMM dashboard and related services
  • Fetch and analyse your posts and insights to generate reports, suggestions and automation
  • Maintain and secure the service (debugging, monitoring, preventing abuse)
  • Communicate with you about updates, issues or support requests

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal basis (for UK / EU users)

Where applicable (e.g. under UK GDPR), we process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interests – to maintain and improve our tools and protect against misuse.
  • Consent – when you explicitly connect your social accounts or opt in to specific features.

You can revoke access at any time by disconnecting COTMM's permissions in your Facebook / Instagram settings.

9. TikTok data we use

When you log in to TikTok Studio with TikTok, we request only the permissions needed for that tool to work:

  • Basic profile information – your TikTok open_id, display name and avatar, so TikTok Studio can show you who is signed in.
  • Access tokens – used only to call TikTok's API on your behalf, so you can publish videos you choose to upload. We never use your token to post anything automatically or without your action.

We do not request or access your TikTok followers, your existing videos, direct messages, or any TikTok data beyond what is listed above.

Your TikTok access token and basic profile information are stored only inside an encrypted, HttpOnly session cookie in your browser, for the length of your session, encrypted before being written and unreadable by JavaScript running on the page. We do not store your TikTok access token, profile information or video content in any database or server-side storage. Videos you choose to upload are sent directly from your browser to TikTok's upload servers; we do not keep a copy of your video files. We do not sell or share your TikTok data with any third party, other than TikTok itself as necessary to operate TikTok Studio, or as required by law.

You can disconnect TikTok Studio from your TikTok account at any time from your TikTok account settings, which revokes our access immediately. To request deletion of any TikTok-related data, or confirmation that none is retained, email sskmusic7@gmail.com.

Your use of TikTok, and any data TikTok itself collects, is governed separately by TikTok's own Privacy Policy, available at tiktok.com/legal/page/row/privacy-policy.
